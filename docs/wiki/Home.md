## Welcome to the Flux Wiki!

Flux is a **real-time multilingual customer support CRM** built with Next.js and Supabase. It enables support agents to manage customer support tickets with automatic AI-powered translation — customers write in any language, agents see messages in their preferred language, and replies are automatically translated back before being sent.

The platform bridges the language gap in customer support, ensuring seamless communication between support agents and customers from anywhere in the world.
